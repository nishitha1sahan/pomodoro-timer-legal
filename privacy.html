Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 November 2026 · Hosted and managed by DenJi · Original developer: Crizter

Information processed

Depending on features used, the bot may process Discord user, server, channel, and role IDs, Pomodoro and Deep Focus session information, timestamps, and temporary role snapshots. Operational logs may contain technical details and identifiers.

Why it is processed

This information supports timers, focus sessions, permission restoration, channel exceptions, and troubleshooting.

Storage and service providers

The bot uses Discord for interactions, Botkeep for hosting, and MongoDB Atlas for storage. Information may also appear in configured Discord log channels accessible to authorized server staff.

Retention

A fixed retention period has not yet been established for every record type. Session data and logs may remain until removed by the maintainer or an applicable cleanup process. Ending a focus session does not necessarily delete all related records immediately.

Data requests

For questions about information associated with your account or deletion requests, open a general GitHub issue without posting your Discord ID or other private details. Ask for a private contact method for account-specific information.

Updates

This policy may change as bot functionality or data handling changes. The maintainer should verify these disclosures against the deployed code and actual retention practices.

Contact: GitHub Issues. Do not post private information in public issues.
